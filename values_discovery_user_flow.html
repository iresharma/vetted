Values discovery user flow — how to learn what matters most to each user and recommend their daily 5 profiles

1
Onboarding complete → trigger values quiz
After required fields are saved, surface a short "what matters most?" moment
Entry point copy (shown after profile goes live)
"Your profile is live 🎉 Before we show you your first 5, help us understand what you actually care about — takes 2 minutes."
or skip — defaults applied
2
Values quiz — 5 ranked-choice questions
Each question maps directly to profile fields, revealing what to weight in the algorithm
Q1 — Deal-breakers first
"Pick up to 3 things you could absolutely not compromise on."

deal-breaker
Q2 — Life stage alignment
"Which matters more to you right now — how soon they want to marry, or where they are in their career?"
Marriage timeline Career ambition Both equally
strong signal
Q3 — Family picture
"How much does it matter that they share your family structure preferences?"
Very — must match Somewhat I'm flexible
strong signal
Q4 — Day-to-day compatibility
"Rank these by how much overlap you want with a partner."
Interests & hobbies Weekend vibe Sleep schedule Exercise habits Social media use
lifestyle
Q5 — Geography & roots
"How important is it that they're from the same city, state, or community as you?"
Same city preferred Same state is fine Anywhere in India Open to NRI too
geography
3
Score → weight map
Answers translate directly into field weights for the recommendation engine
Deal-breakers → hard filters
faith, diet, smoking, drinking, wants_children, kundli
Life stage → weight boost ×2
marriage_timeline, employment_type, income_bracket
Family → weight boost ×1.5
family_structure, family_involvement, living_arrangement
Lifestyle → soft score
interests, weekend_vibe, sleep_schedule, exercise_frequency
Q1 deal-breakers are always hard-excluded. Q2–Q5 adjust ranking weights. Unranked fields use neutral weight (1×).
4
First daily 5 delivered
Profiles ranked by personalised weight map, shown with a "why you matched" reason
Show top match reason: "Values similar faith and family involvement"
Show 2nd reason: "Both looking to marry within a year"
Show 3rd reason: "Matching weekend vibe and interests"
Each card gets 1 primary match reason pulled from their highest-weighted field cluster.
5
Passive signal refinement
Behaviour after day 1 continuously updates the weight map — no quiz needed again
Likes / connects → boost weight of fields where the liked profile differed from average
Passes with "why" reason selected → hard penalise that field cluster next cycle
Conversations started → strongest signal; up-weight all fields shared with that person
Result: a self-improving daily 5 Day 1 is values-quiz-driven. By day 7, behaviour has reshaped the weights. By day 30, the algorithm reflects what the user actually responds to — not what they said they wanted.
